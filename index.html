The app will choose the covered network during rain and the normal mapped route when conditions are dry.

Rain-routing logic

  • During rain or a significant rain probability, the planner prefers the covered network.
  • The covered network can pass through D-Spine → B-Dome → LT 3 & 4, rather than treating buildings as barriers.
  • B-Dome, C-Wing and A-Wing are modeled as indoor covered connectors, so a rainy route may travel through them to reach another covered path.
  • When conditions are clear/dry, the normal mapped route is primary and the covered route remains available as the alternative.

The indoor connectors are a student-project routing model based on the marked campus pathways; they are not an official BITS pedestrian-navigation guarantee.
