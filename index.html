How it works

  • The background is a live OpenStreetMap map, not the uploaded screenshot.
  • Normal routing follows the real mapped road network through OSRM.
  • Your manually marked covered paths are overlaid on the real map and treated as covered.
  • Rain / significant rain probability → covered route is selected.
  • Clear / dry weather → normal road route is selected.
  • The other route is shown as an alternative whenever it can be calculated.
