PROJECT NOTE

How the route choice works: the app compares a modeled sheltered/covered route with a modeled open route. Rain or storm conditions increase the open-route risk; very high heat also increases exposure. The lower-risk route becomes primary and the other route is retained as the backup.

Important: this is a student project prototype. The covered/open paths are modeled from campus landmarks and are not an official BITS pedestrian-routing map. Always follow campus access rules and actual walkways.

Campus facts and landmark names are based on BITS Pilani Goa campus information and the published campus map. Weather is fetched from Open-Meteo.
